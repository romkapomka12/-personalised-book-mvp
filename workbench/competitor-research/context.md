## Competitor Groups Observed

### Classic personalised book brands

Examples:

- Wonderbly
- Hooray Heroes
- Librio
- Твоя казка

Typical pattern:

- printed product first
- strong gift positioning
- stable prewritten stories
- name/avatar personalisation
- strong preview experience
- no or limited AI positioning
- no clear digital-first product in many cases

Strengths:

- trust
- predictable quality
- polished UX
- strong emotional gift framing
- broad catalog in mature competitors

Weaknesses and complaints:

- delivery delays
- damaged shipments
- limited editing after order
- limited hairstyle/avatar choices
- little or no digital-only option
- photo-to-character is absent or unclear for many classic brands

### AI personalised book services

Examples:

- Akoni Books
- Lullaby.ink
- Childbook.ai

Typical pattern:

- faster generation
- photo-to-character
- AI text and AI illustrations
- digital price ladder around `$5–$10`
- print price ladder around `$25–$40`
- partial preview before purchase in some cases
- multi-character and style options in stronger products

Strengths:

- speed
- deeper personalisation
- digital-first business model
- clear pricing ladder in some cases
- technology differentiation

Weaknesses and risks:

- quality consistency risk
- character likeness risk
- full-book preview is not always available
- independent reviews may be limited
- some pricing or checkout details are not fully transparent

### Etsy sellers

Examples:

- MagicPictureStory
- WISEYOUTHshop

Typical pattern:

- marketplace discovery
- manual or semi-manual fulfilment
- personal communication with buyer
- approval/revision loop
- higher trust when seller has reviews
- low trust when listing has no sales/reviews

Strengths:

- handmade/custom feeling
- human review
- revision process
- marketplace traffic

Weaknesses:

- high manual workload
- inconsistent quality between sellers
- dependence on Etsy
- slower customer communication loop

## Pricing Observations

- Classic printed books: roughly `£19.99`, `$29.99–$46.99`, `€34.99`, or `1 200–1 500 грн` depending on market and format.
- AI digital products: commonly around `$5–$10`.
- AI printed products: commonly around `$25–$40`.
- Etsy custom print products can be much higher, e.g. `$44.99+`, because they often include manual work and approval.

## Product Positioning Implication

The strongest opportunity is not simply “AI book”. The stronger positioning is:

> A personalised children's story where the child becomes a recognisable hero, with a simple digital-first entry price, clear preview, Ukrainian/English localisation, and optional human quality control before print.

This combines:

- classic brand trust and preview quality
- AI-service speed and deeper personalisation
- Etsy-style human reassurance and revision logic
- local Ukrainian language advantage with future English-market readiness

## Recommended MVP Positioning

For the first website version, position the product as:

> Create a personalised children's book where your child becomes the main character of a warm illustrated adventure.

Supporting points:

- built first for children aged 3–8
- starts with one simple story direction: space adventure
- uses child name, age, interests, language, and photo
- offers preview before final purchase in the future
- starts digital-first to avoid early logistics complexity
- print options can be added later: softcover and hardcover

## Differentiation Ideas To Preserve

- Full preview before payment, or at least a much stronger preview than AI competitors.
- Digital PDF as the first low-friction product.
- Ukrainian + English from the start.
- Human review before print to reduce AI quality and likeness risks.
- Clear privacy explanation for child photos.
- Future expansion into family, couple, wedding, and life-story books after the children's MVP is validated.
