# Context

## Purpose

This workstream preserves a separate research track for the end-to-end creation and production of personalised books. It must be handled in another chat so it does not interrupt the current privacy and request-storage implementation.

## Research Questions

- Where should the book be assembled and laid out?
- How should a recognisable cartoon character be created from a child's photo?
- How can character appearance remain consistent across all pages?
- Which applications or APIs can generate illustrations from the approved story?
- Which steps should remain manual for the first paid books?
- What print-ready format does a publisher or print shop require?
- How should trim size, bleed, safe areas, resolution, colour profile, spine width, binding, and proofing be handled?
- Which PDF standard and export workflow should be used?
- How do commercial-use rights, privacy, and AI-provider terms affect the chosen tools?
- What is the cost and turnaround time for one digital book and one printed copy?

## Scope Boundaries

- Do not select a final tool stack before comparing evidence, output quality, licensing, cost, automation options, and print requirements.
- Compare at least one manual-first workflow and one automation-ready workflow.
- Obtain real print specifications from candidate printers before locking the page template.
- Do not upload real child photos to new AI services until their privacy and retention terms are reviewed.

## Relationship to Product Roadmap

This research will inform:

- v0.5 manual PDF creation
- v0.6 AI story generation
- v0.8 automated PDF generation
- v1.0 AI or template-based illustrations
